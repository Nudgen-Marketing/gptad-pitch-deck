# Customer deck speaker notes

Suggested length: 6–8 minutes. Invite discussion rather than presenting this as a launched advertising platform.

1. **Be part of the answer.** Introduce GPTAd as a workspace being built for conversational advertising. Ask which product or audience the customer wants to discuss.
2. **The shortlist starts with a question.** Explain that customer constraints and comparisons are useful inputs to a brief. Do not claim measured audience migration or guaranteed discovery.
3. **For teams exploring what comes next.** Identify the listener's role: founder, marketer, ecommerce team, or agency. Focus the remainder on that role.
4. **From buying intent to a better brief.** Explain the intended connection between research, competitive context, and creative concepts. These are planned capabilities.
5. **One product, a more relevant story.** Walk from a product brief to customer questions, a message angle, and an ad concept. Ask where the customer's current process becomes difficult.
6. **Meet the need behind the prompt.** The prompts and stages are illustrative. Explore one genuine customer question supplied by the listener instead of treating examples as demand measurements.
7. **Find missing conversations.** Explicitly identify the fictional brands and sample values. A mention rate is a prompt-set observation, not a placement or revenue guarantee.
8. **One brief, two ways to connect.** Contrast creative freedom with peace of mind. Ask which angle better fits the customer's product. Neither concept is a performance prediction or a completed experiment.
9. **Know what to expect.** State that the product is in development, pricing is forthcoming, and GPTAd does not publish campaigns today. Platform support remains to be confirmed. Explain GPTAd's independence from OpenAI.
10. **Bring your brand.** Invite a demo or potential pilot discussion, or an introduction to a relevant team. Request a product, target audience, and customer question to ground that discussion.

## Likely questions

**Can I run ChatGPT ads now?** GPTAd does not publish campaigns today. Supported features and platform access will be confirmed before launch.

**What will it cost?** Pricing is not announced. Plans and access details will be shared at launch.

**Are these competitor results real?** No. The preview uses fictional brands and illustrative figures to explain the intended experience.

**Is this an OpenAI product?** No. GPTAd is independent and is not endorsed by or affiliated with OpenAI.

**What would a pilot involve?** Start with a discussion of the team's goals and workflow. Availability, scope, and access details would be agreed individually.
